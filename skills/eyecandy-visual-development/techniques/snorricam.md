# Snorricam

<!-- SOURCE-GROUNDED -->
## Technique Identity

- **EYECANDY category:** Snorricam
- **Source status:** taxonomy entry verified (category exists at slug); page definition not captured in this build — identity below is derived naming support.
- **Visual purpose:** camera mounted ON the subject's body facing them — subject frame-stable while their own body and the world lurch around them — raw, intimate, destabilizing presence.

<!-- DERIVED -->
## Use When

- Isolation under pressure: character's internal chaos externalized (intoxication, panic, obsession).
- Tension via wrongness: background behaves impossibly relative to a calm face.
- Performance showcase: micro-expressions carry the scene; world reduced to context.
- Fashion/film crossovers: garment behavior against locked torso reads sculptural.

## Avoid When

- Calm/premium serenity contexts — the grammar's DNA is instability.
- Long dialogue scenes (locked face + bobbing frame fatigues fast).
- Product-only shots with no human carrier — the device needs a body.
- Generation workflows that cannot hold faces under frame vibration — check failure tolerance first.

## Required Inputs

- Performer/subject with controllable micro-behavior (breath, gaze, head turns).
- Mount point concept: chest/harness/headband-class (declared as staging, not gear porn).
- Environment content that will swing past at body-motion speed.
- Framing: face-dominant vs torso-and-face.

## Directing Rule

- Subject's HEAD stays near-fixed in frame; the camera inherits every body jolt — so direct the BODY to move and the face to resist: walking bounce, shoulder sway, sudden stops.
- Background motion = body motion translated: each step swings the world opposite.
- Keep face lighting constant while background light strobes/pans — the anchor must be visually trustworthy amid chaos.
- Micro-gestures read HUGE: swallow, blink cadence, jaw set — choreograph them like action beats.
- Duration discipline: 2–5s per take; longer requires an event (stop, turn, collision).

## Storyboard Translation

- **Setup frame:** subject framed per mount spec, environment mid-swing behind, expression baseline established.
- **Transition frame:** peak body-event — background streaked/swung, face unchanged, one micro-gesture landing.
- **Reveal frame:** stop/collision moment — world freezes its swing, subject's reaction becomes the only motion left.

## Image Generation Translation

Keyframe recipe: tight face/torso framing, tack-sharp face, background smeared radially from body pivot, subtle motion cue on clothing edges. Slight wide-angle distortion at frame corners sells body-mounted optics without caricature.

## Video Generation Translation

Specify: "camera fixed relative to subject's torso, facing [face/chest]; subject walks/moves causing rhythmic frame oscillation synced to steps; face remains stable within frame; background swings opposite each movement." Declare duration, gait rhythm, one planned micro-gesture beat, end event. Forbid: smooth gimbal feel (the imperfection is the point) but cap shake amplitude so identity holds.

## Failure Modes

- Face melts under vibration (identity loss) → reduce oscillation amplitude; shorten take; re-anchor facial reference.
- Whole frame shakes uniformly (reads like bad handheld, not body-mount) → ensure subject region moves LEAST — differential motion is the signature.
- Background too legible (no sense of attachment) → increase background angular velocity cues.
- Monotony: same bounce loop → schedule variation: pace change, direction change, stop.

## Preservation Rules

Face identity is the entire asset — treat any drift as hard fail; keep amplitude low enough that facial geometry never smears. Wardrobe: collar/straps near lens may distort — keep brand wardrobe elements out of extreme foreground. Logo placement: chest-print logos sit close to lens → declare flatness/no-warp on logo zone. Motif link: motif may hang around subject's neck/wrist so it jolts WITH them (verb-Match through shared motion). Downgrade path: handheld follow-cam with heavy subject stabilization in post-grammar terms when body-mount generation fails.
