# Speed Ramp

<!-- SOURCE-GROUNDED -->
## Technique Identity

- **EYECANDY category:** Speed Ramp
- **Source status:** taxonomy entry verified (category exists at slug); page definition not captured in this build — identity below is derived naming support.
- **Visual purpose:** time itself accelerates or brakes mid-shot — real-time action snaps into slow motion (or reverse) exactly on the beat that matters, bending duration around emphasis.

<!-- DERIVED -->
## Use When

- Escalation into impact: approach at speed, snap to slow at the apex (kick, splash, pour, spin).
- Elegance: mundane motion becomes sculptural in slowed windows (fabric, liquid, hair).
- Compression: boring travel accelerated between two hero moments.
- Beat-synced music video grammar where one physical moment must own several musical hits.

## Avoid When

- Every shot ramps (time loses all meaning; audiences stop feeling the bend).
- Dialogue/lip-sync moments (slow-mo breaks voice sync logic unless stylized).
- Product information moments: label reading needs real-time dwell.
- Slow sections already lacking pace — ramping adds shape only when a rhythm exists to break.

## Required Inputs

- The action with ONE identifiable apex moment (the frame where time should bend).
- Pre-apex speed and post-apex speed targets.
- Beat map (which musical/story hits align with which speed state).
- Motion content rich enough to reward slowing (particles, fabric, splashes) — slow-mo of a static subject is dead air.

## Directing Rule

- Shoot/direct the FULL action in continuous real-time physics; ramping is applied around a preserved apex — never direct an action "in slow motion" from scratch.
- Ramp curve: accelerate INTO the apex cut-point, hold slow through the payoff window (~0.5–1.5s screen time), release back to real-time (or cut while still slow).
- Anchor the apex to the strongest beat; the transition zones ride secondary beats.
- Camera behavior stays constant across the ramp (no simultaneous move changes) — time bends, space doesn't.
- Reverse ramps (slow→fast) work for releases: held tension snapping into resolution.

## Storyboard Translation

- **Setup frame:** action beginning at readable real-time speed, apex not yet visible.
- **Transition frame:** THE APEX — the single frame worth printing; board it like key art with all particles/fabric at maximum drama.
- **Reveal frame:** aftermath at released speed OR continued slow window if the next cut continues the freeze-feel.

## Image Generation Translation

The apex frame is the deliverable: peak deformation (splash crown, fabric bloom), environment cues frozen mid-flight, subject expression at maximum commitment. This still doubles as poster art regardless of whether video follows.

## Video Generation Translation

Specify: full action described at natural speed, then time mapping — "motion proceeds at normal rate until [apex event], slows to approximately quarter-rate through [payoff window], returns to normal by [end event]." Declare camera locked-or-steady across ramp. For generation models without native ramp control, direct the apex moment AS slow-motion capture and join via hard cut on beat (declare the seam).

## Failure Modes

- Interpolated mush during ramp zones (limbs smear between speeds) → keep ramp transitions short (<8 frames each side); slow window fully committed rather than gradual mush.
- Apex drifts off-beat → re-time ramp to audio after generation; never stretch the whole clip hoping.
- Nothing rewards the slowdown (thin motion) → enrich action with particle/fluid/fabric elements before applying the device.
- Physics betrayal in slow window (hair moves faster than splash) → declare global time scale affects everything equally.

## Preservation Rules

Subject identity across speed states: no face morphing between fast/slow renders. Product: liquid pours and product interactions keep brand-correct physics (viscosity, color); label legible at apex if the apex features it. Wardrobe continuity. Motif link: motif element gets the richest slow-window treatment (verb-Match — same motif, magnified attention). Downgrade path: shoot apex-only slow motion joined by hard cuts to real-time coverage.
