# Match Motion

<!-- SOURCE-GROUNDED -->
## Technique Identity

- **EYECANDY category:** Match motion (registry spelling; site renders as "Match motion")
- **Source status:** taxonomy entry verified (category exists at slug); page definition not captured in this build — identity below is derived naming support.
- **Visual purpose:** a movement vector survives the cut — spin answers spin, fall answers fall, camera whip becomes subject motion — so two different scenes feel physically continuous.

<!-- DERIVED -->
## Use When

- Motif linkage when the motif is kinetic, not formal: recurring gestures, rotations, descents.
- Energy handoff between scenes with different subjects/locations but same physics.
- Dance/performance films: body motion transfers into object motion and back.
- Impact: accelerating motion across the cut makes both scenes hit harder.

## Avoid When

- Either side's motion is subtle — matched vectors must be unmistakable or the cut reads random.
- Motion direction contradicts screen-direction continuity rules of the surrounding sequence without intent.
- The two motions share nothing but speed category (slow + slow is not a match).
- Overuse: kinetic matching every cut turns choreography into noise.

## Required Inputs

- Outgoing motion: what moves, which vector/direction, its peak velocity moment.
- Incoming motion built to receive that vector (same axis class: rotational/vertical/horizontal/push).
- Cut timing point relative to motion phase (usually just past peak).
- Screen-direction map for the sequence.

## Directing Rule

- Classify the outgoing vector precisely: axis (X/Y/Z), type (rotation/translation/scale-change), phase position.
- Time the cut within ±3 frames of the outgoing motion's peak-energy moment.
- Incoming scene opens already in motion on the same vector class — never from rest; rest kills the handoff.
- Preserve direction sense where possible: clockwise→clockwise, downward→downward. Deliberate reversal = separate device (declare it).
- Camera may change completely across the cut; only the MOTION continues.

## Storyboard Translation

- **Setup frame:** mover A mid-gesture at readable energy (skirt lifting, car turning in, hand rising) — the frame implies the arc's continuation.
- **Transition frame:** board as pair again — "A-last: rotation at peak / B-first: different object, same rotation at peak."
- **Reveal frame:** new mover completes the inherited gesture into ITS payoff (spinning fan slows to reveal product; fall lands somewhere meaningful).

## Image Generation Translation

Freeze both movers at equivalent phase: same lean angle, same blur direction, same limb/fold geometry class. Diptych rule applies. For single key art, choose the incoming frame — it must contain enough motion cue (blur streaks, fabric lift, dust kick) to imply the inherited vector.

## Video Generation Translation

Two shots, one hard cut at matched phase. Declare per shot: moving element, vector axis and sense, phase at cut ("cut as skirt reaches 45° lift"), incoming element already rotating/moving at entry, and post-cut resolution (motion decays or accelerates toward next beat). Speed ramp across the cut allowed if both sides ramp together.

## Failure Modes

- Phase mismatch (incoming starts from standstill) → explicitly forbid "starts moving" language; demand in-motion first frame.
- Vector inversion by accident → restate direction sense in both shot specs.
- Motion morphs subject mid-vector (A's arm becomes B's fan blade continuously) → hard cut semantics; no crossfade.
- Physics whiplash: matched speeds feel wrong because masses differ hugely → adjust incoming speed to plausible momentum transfer, not literal equality.

## Preservation Rules

Mover identity locked per side (no blending across the cut). Faces: if performer hands off to object, performer's final pose reads clean before the seam. Product surfaces: if the product receives the motion, it enters already moving — never spins up on camera (reads like a turntable error). Spatial continuity intentionally resets; declare it. Motif link: verb-Match on the kinetic property; log the vector class used so consecutive match-motions alternate axes. Downgrade path: sound-design bridge (whoosh continuing over cut) when visual matching fails generation.
