# Dolly Zoom

<!-- SOURCE-GROUNDED -->
## Technique Identity

- **EYECANDY category:** Dolly Zoom
- **Source status:** taxonomy entry verified (category exists at slug); page definition not captured in this build — identity below is derived naming support.
- **Visual purpose:** camera moves toward while lens widens (or reverse) — subject stays identical size while the world behind stretches or compresses — vertigo without moving the protagonist.

<!-- DERIVED -->
## Use When

- Tension: realization moment — world shifts around an unchanged subject.
- Scale-perception: depth itself is the message (corridor becomes infinite; room becomes claustrophobic).
- Impact punct: short inverted dolly-zoom as a "record-scratch" of space.
- Psychological turn in narrative shorts/brand drama.

## Avoid When

- More than once per film/section — its power is rarity; twice reads as a tic.
- Busy backgrounds: the effect needs readable background geometry to stretch against.
- Subject must move during the effect (two simultaneous motion systems cancel it).
- Product hero shots where background distortion distracts from form reading.

## Required Inputs

- Locked subject at fixed screen size (declare the framing: e.g., chest-up centered).
- Background with strong linear/perspective structure (corridor, rows, skyline, tiles).
- Direction: push-in + widen (background recedes/stretches — dread, awe) or pull-out + tighten (background swells/crushes — paranoia, pressure).
- Duration and end-state.

## Directing Rule

- Subject occupies IDENTICAL frame area at start and end — that constancy is the entire trick; declare it explicitly.
- Move along the lens axis only; no lateral drift.
- Background must contain converging lines so stretching/compressing is legible.
- Speed profile: slow build then committed acceleration through the middle third, settle at end state and HOLD ≥1s.
- End state is a composition: choose the final background distortion deliberately (wall of sky / tunnel of doorways).

## Storyboard Translation

- **Setup frame:** subject framed per lock spec; background in natural perspective (baseline).
- **Transition frame:** mid-effect — subject unchanged, background visibly warped vs baseline (board both mini-frames for comparison).
- **Reveal frame:** end-state background extreme; subject still pixel-consistent with setup frame.

## Image Generation Translation

Cannot be a single true still — generate the two endpoint states as a pair with identical subject crop. The pair proves the effect. For one keyframe, use mid-or-end state with subtly wrong perspective (background lines too convergent) to imply unease — flag it as stylized, not documentary.

## Video Generation Translation

Specify: "camera dollies [in/out] on axis while focal length [narrows/widens] such that subject size in frame remains constant throughout; background perspective stretches/compresses accordingly." Declare duration (2–4s), hold at end, locked focus on subject. Forbid subject movement and lateral drift.

## Failure Modes

- Subject size creeps during move (the classic generation error — effect becomes a normal push-in) → restate size-lock every prompt revision; check first/last frames for equal subject height.
- Background warps into liquid abstraction instead of geometric stretch → name concrete background objects whose lines should bend.
- Focus hunts between subject/background → lock focus declaration.
- Effect fires without narrative cause → tie trigger to a story beat (door opens, truth lands).

## Preservation Rules

Subject identity: face unchanged across the entire effect; no expression-morph allowed by the warp. Product: keep product OUT of the distorting background plane; if product is subject, it holds perfectly rigid. Wardrobe stable. Spatial continuity: the distortion is declared subjective (POV pressure), geography restores after unless the sequence ends there. Motif link: motif may live in the stretching background (repeated motif elements multiply during stretch = verb-Mutation). Downgrade path: static frame + background plate swap (before/after backgrounds, same subject plate).
